# Dino Dash — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 27, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dino Dash ("the App"), a casual dinosaur running game, handles information when you play. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the game simple and enjoyable.

**Developer:** Dino Dash developer  
**Contact:** black2020panther@gmail.com

## 2. Information We Collect

The current version of Dino Dash is designed to operate without collecting or transmitting personal information to us.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or location.

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

Dino Dash stores your best score locally on your device so that you can track your progress between sessions.

This score is stored using local application storage. It is not intentionally transmitted to the developer or a third party by the game.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data may remove the locally stored score.

## 4. Advertising and Analytics

Dino Dash is intended to be free to play without advertisements.

The current intended version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not use the game to build advertising profiles or track your activity across other apps.

## 5. Sharing of Information

The current intended version does not send gameplay information or personal information to the developer or third parties. We do not sell or rent personal information.

If future versions introduce services that collect or share data, this policy will be updated to explain those practices before or when they are introduced, as applicable.

## 6. Internet and Device Permissions

The game is designed to work offline and does not need access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos for its core gameplay.

The Android operating system may process technical information as part of normal device operation. This policy does not claim that the developer receives such information.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Dino Dash is a casual game that may appeal to a broad audience. The App is not intended to collect personal information from children.

If the game is distributed to children or is designed to target children, additional applicable requirements and Google Play Families policies must be followed.

## 8. Data Security and Retention

The best score is stored locally within the App's application data. We do not maintain a developer-operated database of player scores in the current intended version.

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. Any revised version will be published at the privacy policy URL associated with the App.
